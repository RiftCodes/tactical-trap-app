Tactical Traps App – Recent Improvements

Hello! Here’s a quick summary of the latest updates we made to your Bluetooth Lock app:

1. **Easier Device Connection**
- The app now automatically tries to fix Bluetooth issues if your lock isn’t found—no need to turn Bluetooth off and on yourself.
- If you cancel a device search, the app immediately stops searching (no more background activity).

2. **Better, Cleaner Design**
- Device search and connection screens are now perfectly centered and look great on all phones.
- The menu and options screens are scrollable, and the close button is always easy to find.
- The connected device card always shows the pencil icon on the right, with the device name on the left.

3. **Smarter, Safer Reconnect**
- If you’ve connected to a lock before, the app will offer to reconnect automatically when you open it again.
- If you manually disconnect, the app will always ask for your PIN the next time (for your security).
- The reconnect prompt always shows the correct lock name or serial number, so you know which device you’re connecting to.

4. **Clearer Messages**
- All popups and prompts now use clear, friendly language.
- You’ll always know what’s happening and what to do next.

These changes make the app much easier, safer, and more enjoyable to use. If you have any questions or want more changes, just let us know!
